# How to prepare for GSoC

Google Summer of Code (GSoC) is a global, online program focused on bringing new contributors into open source software development. GSoC contributors work with an open source organization on a 12+ week programming project under the guidance of mentors.

## Finding the right organization

The key to a successful GSoC application is finding an organization that aligns with your interests and tech stack. 

1. Look through past years' organizations.
2. Identify 2-3 organizations that use languages you are comfortable with.
3. Join their mailing lists, Discord, or Slack channels.
4. Introduce yourself and ask how you can start contributing.

## The Proposal

Your proposal is your blueprint. It should include:
- A clear timeline
- Implementation details
- Potential roadblocks and solutions
- Information about your past contributions

Start early, and don't hesitate to ask your mentors for feedback before the final submission!
