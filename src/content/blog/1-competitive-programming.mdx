# Getting started with competitive programming

Competitive programming is a mind sport usually held over the Internet or a local network, involving participants trying to program according to provided specifications. Contestants are referred to as sport programmers.

## Why Competitive Programming?

1. **Problem Solving**: It enhances your logical thinking and problem-solving skills drastically.
2. **Interviews**: Most top tech companies rely on Data Structures and Algorithms during their technical interviews.
3. **Community**: You get to be part of an amazing global community of developers.

## Where to start?

- **Codeforces**: The best platform for active contests.
- **LeetCode**: Great for interview preparation.
- **AtCoder**: Known for very high-quality educational problems.

### Core Topics to cover:
- Basic Data Structures (Arrays, Strings, Stacks, Queues)
- Sorting & Searching
- Dynamic Programming
- Graph Theory (BFS, DFS, Shortest Paths)

Happy coding!
