# ACM SVNIT Hackathon 2024 Recap

What a weekend it has been! Our flagship event, DotSlash 8.0, concluded this Sunday, and we are still buzzing with the energy that 1200+ participants brought to the SVNIT campus.

## The Numbers

- **1200+** Participants
- **250+** Teams
- **100+** Completed Projects
- **36** Hours of non-stop coding
- **₹5,000,00** in prizes distributed

## Winning Themes

The most popular tracks this year were Web3, AI for Good, and EdTech. The winning team built an incredible accessibility tool using computer vision to translate sign language in real-time.

A huge thank you to our sponsors, mentors, and the organizing committee for making this happen. We'll see you next year!
